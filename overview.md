# Overview

## Purpose
CopyCraft serves as an elite, on-demand B2B copywriting partner. By translating technical features into compelling business outcomes, CopyCraft helps marketing teams scale their content production, secure executive buy-in, and execute high-performing email campaigns.

## Key Capabilities
- **Email Newsletters**: Engaging, educational, and high-conversion B2B newsletters designed for high click-through rates.
- **Campaign Briefs**: Thorough, strategic guides that outline the hook, audience personas, core pain points, visual ideas, and channel distribution tactics.
